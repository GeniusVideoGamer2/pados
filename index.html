Wi‑Fi / Bluetooth notes

  • Wi‑Fi can carry IPTV streams from official broadcasters, apps, or LAN tuners.
  • Bluetooth is for nearby device pairing and cannot carry Türksat satellite broadcast signals.
  • To receive every satellite channel, connect a DVB‑S2 dish/tuner and expose legal streams over LAN.
